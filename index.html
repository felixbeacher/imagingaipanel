Sector Outlook Summary (12-18 Month View)

Moderately Bullish

The medical imaging AI sector is transitioning from algorithm validation to large-scale clinical integration. Structural workforce shortages in radiology and growing scan backlogs provide strong demand resilience against wider macroeconomic headwinds. High central bank interest rates continue to extend capital purchasing cycles, but adoption is sustained by clear reimbursement pathway expansions and integrated platform partnerships with major OEMs.

Primary Growth Driver
Severe diagnostic workforce deficits encouraging health trusts to mandate automated triage tools.
Key Headwind
Elevated cost of capital dampening hospital CapEx budgets and slowing enterprise software deals.
Strategic Catalyst
Expansion of dedicated CPT and national health service reimbursement tariffs for AI-assisted scans.
